# Proxy Vietnam City Guide — Legal Research Note (2026-08-16)

This note is a product/legal research input, not legal advice.

## Current official-source observations

- Vietnam Tourism Law 09/2017/QH14 Article 58 distinguishes international, domestic and on-site tour guides and sets practice conditions, including guide cards and specified employment/membership/guide-contract relationships.
- Article 59 sets card qualification requirements; international guide qualification includes foreign-language proficiency requirements.
- Article 65 lists tour-guide duties, including following the assigned / contracted program, informing travelers of program/services, supporting traveler safety, wearing the guide card and carrying assignment/program documents where applicable.
- Decree 168/2017/NĐ-CP is listed by the National Legal Database as still effective as of the research date.

## Product consequence

Do not assume that changing the consumer label from “tour guide” to “city companion” removes tourism-law obligations. Classification must follow actual scope of service.

## Launch questions for Vietnamese counsel

1. Exact boundary between casual paid local companionship and regulated tourism guiding.
2. When Proxy itself would be treated as a travel-service / guide-service / intermediary business.
3. Contract / assignment structure needed for licensed guides sourced through Proxy.
4. International traveler flows and guide credential scope.
5. Insurance and consumer liability obligations.
6. Merchant commissions / itinerary recommendations and anti-forced-shopping controls.
